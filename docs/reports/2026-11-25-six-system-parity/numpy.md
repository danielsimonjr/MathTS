## 2. Method

1. **Inventory**: [`inventory.csv`](data/numpy-inventory.csv) (705 rows, one-line docstring description, source column).
2. **Mapping**: each item was mapped by hand to MathTS (`scripts/table_core.txt`, `scripts/table_sub.txt`, plus rules for the five orthogonal-polynomial families), then `scripts/build_mapping.py` and `scripts/write_outputs.py` produced [`mapping.csv`](data/numpy-mapping.csv).
3. **Two methods for every "missing" row**: (a) a runtime export check against all runtime names and methods, case- and underscore-insensitive (`linspace`, `lin_space`, `linSpace`, `.linspace` all count), and (b) a source scan of every exported and non-exported declaration in the repo. Any hit was reviewed. Non-exported helpers found this way are noted in the row: `broadcastArrays`, `copysign`, `ldexp` (core), `ifft2`, `polyFromRoots`, the private `_shuffle` and `polyPow`, and the `EULER_GAMMA` constant. These rows stay `missing` because users can't reach them, but each one is a one-line export fix.
4. **Runtime differential checks** (`verify/`): 577 cases, 28 of them statistical sampler tests. They cover **320 of the 705 rows (45.4%)** and 318 of the 319 full/partial rows. The only full/partial row without a runtime check is `piecewise`, which is a symbolic CAS function. Tolerances are recorded per case.
5. **Status rules**: `full` means the core NumPy behavior works and the remaining differences are only keyword extras (`out=`, `where=`, `dtype=`, `order=`). `partial` means an everyday option, axis support, N-d support or the result shape/convention differs. `n/a` covers Python-only syntax helpers (`r_`, `s_`, `nditer`, `vectorize`, ...) and dtype-promotion machinery.

Conventions that look like bugs but are deliberate or inherited from mathjs, so they aren't counted as bugs. They're listed so the build batches can decide whether to add NumPy-style options:

- `round` rounds half away from zero (NumPy rounds half to even).
- `diag(v,k)` returns an n×(n+k) matrix.
- `resize` pads with zeros instead of repeating.
- `linearInterp`/`interpolate` extrapolate (NumPy clamps).
- `cov`/`corrcoef` default to `rowvar=false`, and `cov(x,y)` returns a scalar.
- `setUnion` keeps first-seen order.
- `mod(x,0)` returns `x`.
- `asin(2)` lands on the other side of the branch cut.
- `pow(-8,1/3)` returns a complex root.
- `M.eig` returns eigenvectors as rows.
- `qr` returns a complete-shape R.
- `polyval`/`polyadd`/`polyder`/`polyFit` use ascending coefficients, which is `numpy.polynomial` order, not legacy `np.poly*` order.
- `companion` uses the first-row form.

## 5. Top gaps, ranked

1. **Grids and ranges (P0):** `linspace`, `logspace`, `geomspace`, `meshgrid` (`mgrid`/`ogrid` equivalents) and `*_like` creators are all missing. `identity` has no diagonal offset `k`.
2. **Elementwise selection (P0):** `where` (both the 1-arg and 3-arg forms), elementwise `maximum`/`minimum`/`fmax`/`fmin`, and `select` are missing. `clip` is 1-D only.
3. **Arg-reductions and searching (P0):** `argmax`, `argmin`, `argsort`, `searchsorted`, `nanargmax`/`nanargmin`, `lexsort` and `argpartition` are missing.
4. **Boolean reductions and tolerance tests (P0):** `all`, `any`, `isclose` and `allclose` are missing, as are `isinf`, `isposinf`/`isneginf` and `nan_to_num`.
5. **Stacking and splitting (P0/P1):** `vstack`, `hstack`, `dstack`, `block`, `split`/`array_split`/`hsplit`/`vsplit`, `tile`, `repeat`, `append`, `insert`, `delete`, `expand_dims`, `moveaxis`, `swapaxes`, `atleast_*` and `broadcast_to`/`broadcast_arrays` are missing. `stack`, `flip`, `roll` and `pad` exist only on `Tensor`.
6. **Polynomial roots of any degree (P0):** `polynomialRoot` stops at degree 3, `realRoots` returns only real roots, and there's no companion-eigenvalue `roots`. `polyFromRoots` is internal and `polyint`/`polysub` are missing.
7. **The Generator API (P0):** there's no Generator object with its own stream, `dist.sample()` is unseeded, `integers` has no `endpoint`, `choice` has no `replace=False`, and `shuffle`/`permutation`/`permuted` are missing. 12 samplers are missing: laplace, logistic, cauchy, geometric, vonmises, zipf, power, logseries, noncentral χ²/F, multinomial and multivariate hypergeometric.
8. **FFT options and N-d inverses (P0/P1):** `fft` has no `n`/`norm`/`axis` options. `ifft2` isn't exported. `ifftn`, `rfft2`/`irfft2`/`rfftn`/`irfftn` and `hfft`/`ihfft` are missing. `fftshift` has no `axes`.
9. **Linalg options (P0):** no batched (stacked) `det`/`inv`/`solve`; `svd` lacks `full_matrices`; `qr` lacks `mode`; `cond` takes no `p`; `norm` has no `axis`/`keepdims`/`nuc`; `trace` has no offset; `lstsq` returns only x; `outer` is flat; `einsum` takes no subscript strings; `multi_dot` and `tensorinv` are missing.
10. **nan reductions (P1):** `nansum`, `nanprod`, `nanmax`, `nanmin`, `nancumsum`, `nancumprod`, `nanquantile` and `nanpercentile` are missing; nanmean, nanmedian, nanstd and nanvar exist.
11. **Counting and binning (P1):** `bincount`, `digitize`, `isin`, `unique` with `return_index`/`inverse`/`counts` (plus `unique_all`/`unique_inverse`), `count_nonzero`, `nonzero`/`argwhere`/`flatnonzero`, `histogram` with edges/range/density/weights, and `histogram2d`/`dd` are missing.
12. **Indexing helpers (P1/P2):** `take_along_axis`, `put_along_axis`, `put`, `putmask`, `place`, `choose`, `unravel_index`, `ravel_multi_index`, `ix_`, `diag_indices`, `tril_indices`/`triu_indices` and `fill_diagonal` are missing.
13. **dtypes (P1):** `Tensor` is float64-only. There's no float32/int/bool/complex array storage and no `astype`, `finfo` or `iinfo`; complex exists only as a scalar class.
14. **Misc elementwise math (P1/P2):** `exp2`, `reciprocal`, `floor_divide`, `fmod`, `divmod`, `rint` (half-even), `heaviside`, `signbit`, `nextafter`, `spacing`, `frexp`, `modf`, elementwise `logaddexp`/`logaddexp2` and elementwise `hypot` are missing. `copysign` and `ldexp` exist but aren't exported.
15. **`numpy.polynomial` (P2, but large: 159 rows):** there are no `Polynomial`/`Chebyshev`/`Legendre`/`Laguerre`/`Hermite`/`HermiteE` classes and no series arithmetic (`*add/sub/mul/div/pow/der/int/val/vander/fit/roots/companion/fromroots/2poly`). Only basis evaluators (`chebyshevT`, `legendreP`, `hermiteH`, `laguerreL`), Gauss nodes (`roots*`) and Chebyshev/Legendre fits exist.

## 6. Where MathTS is ahead of NumPy

These were checked at runtime; exact answers in parentheses.

- **Overflow and underflow-safe reductions:** `mean([1e308,1e308])` → `1e308` and `std([1e200,-1e200])` → `1e200`, where NumPy gives `inf`. `norm([1e200,1e200])` → `1.414e200` where NumPy overflows. `hypot(1e-200,1e-200)` matches.
- **Correctly rounded running sums:** `cumsum([1e16,1,-1e16,1])` → `[1e16,1e16,1,2]`, where NumPy's naive cumsum gives `[1e16,1e16,0,1]`.
- **Ill-conditioned polynomial fits:** `polyFit` on x = 1000..1010, y = x³ gives exactly `[0,0,0,1]`. NumPy `np.polyfit` returns `[2.0012, -0.00597, 5.9e-6, 1.0]` with a RankWarning.
- **Breadth beyond NumPy's scope** (not in this inventory, and NumPy has no equivalent): arbitrary precision (`BigNumber`), exact `Fraction`, units with exact scales, forward and reverse autodiff (`grad`, `jacobian`, `Tape`, `DualTensor`), a symbolic CAS, matrix functions (`matrixExpm`/`Logm`/`Sqrtm`/`Schur`, fractional `matrixPower`), probability distribution objects with pdf/cdf/quantile/mean/variance, and WASM/WebGPU backends.
- **Named-index tensors:** `Tensor` contraction by index labels (`Index`, `contract`, `tensorSolve` with named axes) has no NumPy counterpart.

## 8. What wasn't verified

- **385 of 705 rows have no runtime differential check.** 354 of them are `missing` rows, which were checked by runtime export plus source declaration scan only, so nothing could be run. 30 are `n/a`. `piecewise` was checked by name only, since it's a CAS function rather than NumPy's array `piecewise`.
- **Options inside "partial" rows were sampled, not exhausted.** For example, `axis`/`keepdims` were tried on `sum`, `mean`, `max`, `min`, `prod`, `median`, `cumsum`, `diff`, `std`, `variance` and `quantileSeq`, but not on every reduction. `dtype=`, `out=`, `where=` and `order=` weren't tested anywhere, because MathTS has no equivalent concept.
- **Complex-valued inputs** were tested for `abs`, `re`/`im`/`conj`/`arg`, `fft`, Hermitian `eigs`, `dot` (vdot) and complex multiplication only. Complex input to reductions, sorting, linalg factorizations other than eig/eigs, and polynomial roots wasn't tested.
- **Samplers were tested by distribution shape only** (KS / pmf at alpha 0.001, n = 20000), not bit-for-bit, because NumPy's PCG64 streams can't be reproduced in MathTS. `exponentialDist` (rate) and `paretoDist` (Pareto I) parameterisations were mapped to NumPy's by hand; that mapping was confirmed by the KS tests.
- **`tensorSolve` wasn't checked numerically.** Its axis convention differs from `np.linalg.tensorsolve`, and the NumPy-style call throws. The working convention wasn't explored.
- **Performance, large sizes, WASM/GPU dispatch paths, BigNumber/Fraction inputs, and Matrix-vs-Array return types** were not compared, except where they affect values. The largest input tested was n = 1001 for reductions and n = 1000 for fft.
- **Error message text** wasn't compared. `both-throw` cases check only that both sides raise.
- **Description text** in [`inventory.csv`](data/numpy-inventory.csv) is the first docstring line from the installed NumPy, not hand-written.
