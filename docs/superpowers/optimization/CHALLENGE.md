## Checks that passed unchanged

- **Reproducibility.** Two consecutive generator runs produce byte-identical `inventory.json` and `README.md`.
- **`paired` evidence and thresholds.** All 8 WebGPU pairings resolve and dispatch through the named `*GpuDispatch`/`gpuMatrixBackend` (code and runtime); 20 wasm and 20 workpool `paired` rows sampled — every anchor resolves, every band matches `policy.ts` / `ComputePool.ts`. 10 `unsure` rows sampled — reasons are honest ("no measurement on record").
- **`policy.ts` bands.** The generator's `POLICY_BANDS` equals `WASM_DISPATCH_POLICY` on all 14 keys exactly.
- **Worker-pool thresholds.** `matmul 7_077_888` (`ComputePool.ts:381`), hypothesis `4_096`, `erfc 100_000`, `sampleChunk 100_000`, every reduction/elementwise op `'never'`; the M1 legend matches, and `docs/benchmarks/ws-r-matmul-decision.json` exists and backs it.
- **WebGPU thresholds.** `GPU_MIN_ELEMENTS = 65_536` (`gpu/src/flag.ts:39`); `FFT_GPU_MIN_ELEMENTS = 262_144` (`functions/src/gpu/fft-gpu.ts:81`).
- **Dual-pair contract.** Every `dualSetup.wasm: existing` row has an `asImpl.file` on disk that declares its kernel; every `dualSetup.gpu: existing` row has an exported dispatcher (runtime: all 9 GPU exports are functions). `DETERMINISTIC_WASM = {array_abs_ptr}` (`policy.ts:66-74`), `F32_REL_TOL`/`REAL_GPU` in `functions/tests/helpers/gpu-hardware.ts`, `enableGpu`/`implicitGpuAllowed` in `functions/src/gpu/implicit.ts`, `wasmPolicyAllows` in `policy.ts`.
- **functions.md Accel edits.** `sin` band (F4), the Arithmetic `—` cells and `pow` cell, the Hypothesis `parallel (n ≥ 4096)` column, the "2 of 215" prose, and the `parallelConv`/`XCorr`/`AutoCorr` nameless-50k paragraph all match the code (`signal.ts:352`, `ComputePool.ts`). `functions.html` mirrors `functions.md` in its `<script id="md">` block; the check regenerates both.
- **Docs gates.** `bun run docs:functions:check` → "All 10 generated doc blocks are up to date. functions surface reconciles: all 1433 shipped exports have a source origin." `bun run lint` and `prettier --check` → clean.

## Findings I considered and rejected (no change)

- **"not-worth-it" rows that look parallel/GPU-friendly.** I hunted for elementwise, reductions, GEMM, FFT, sorting and big-sample statistics marked `not-worth-it` without a measurement. The bulk are backed by named benchmarks. Two I checked hard and left alone: `sort`/`*Select` (sort fan-out measured memory-bound; argsort 0.92–1.13×) and the reductions (`sum`/`mean`/`variance` 0.09–1.19×). The 19 rows whose reason says "unmeasured" are all WebGPU sort/select/set ops, where the reason also gives an independent f32-index-fragility argument; I sharpened the wording (F7) rather than reclassify, because reclassifying on an unmeasured guess is the error this inventory exists to prevent.
- **`asStandalone: existing` files that import something.** All 21 "standalone" files are genuinely import-free (independent walk: 29 files, 8 dependent). No `existing` cites a dependent file.
- **M9 / M10.** Confirmed: `matrixPowNumber` does route through `backendManager.multiply` (so `pow`'s "—" cell was an under-claim), and `eig-wasm.ts`'s header says the AS eig/svd kernels were removed 2026-07-01 with `tensorEigWasm` merely awaiting the JS `eigWasm`.
- **M6 / M7.** Confirmed: `runChainLifecycle`'s object-type parameter is at `functions/src/gpu/elementwise-gpu.ts:314`, `getGpuDevice` at `:255`, dispatchers at `:471`/`:560`; and the generated wasm-pairing really says "21 / 0" of 23 with two `unknown` rows while its own Phase-6 note contradicts the trailing note.
