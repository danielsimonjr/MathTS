# Domain Function Gap Analysis — Math & Scientific Coverage

> **Source of truth:** the `docs/reference/functions.md` export index (744 exports,
> generated by `tools/generate-functions-reference.mjs`). Every "missing" claim in
> this report was verified absent against the live export surface — a probe of 70+
> candidate names confirmed none are exported under an alternate name (see
> Methodology). This complements, rather than duplicates, the two existing reports:
> `GAP_ANALYSIS_BRIDGES_AND_MATH_FUNCTIONS.md` (package-level bridges B1–B10 +
> mathjs-API parity) and `FUNCTION_GAPS.md` (implementation slices). Those analyze
> *plumbing between packages*; **this report analyzes mathematical/scientific
> completeness within domains, and connector ("bridge") functions between domains.**

**Date:** 2026-06-30 · **Bar for comparison:** SciPy / NumPy / SymPy / mathjs as the
reference "complete" scientific-computing surface.

> **Implementation status — Wave A ✅ landed (2026-06-30).** 18 functions added,
> all verified against a SciPy/NumPy oracle (21/21 to 1e-9): `gmean`, `hmean`,
> `moment`, `skewness`, `kurtosis`, `iqr`, `sem`, `zscore`, `cov`, `corrcoef`
> (`functions/src/descriptive-stats.ts`) and `clamp`, `sigmoid`, `logsumexp`,
> `softmax`, `cumprod`, `cummax`, `cummin`, `cumtrapz` (`functions/src/numeric-extra.ts`).
> Each is a composition over existing primitives (`mean`/`std`/`variance`/`sum`/
> `max`/`quantileSeq`) — no reduction was re-implemented.
>
> **Wave B — in progress (2026-06-30).** Bridge **C4** (standalone distribution
> surface) + low-complexity tests landed, all SciPy-verified (13/13): `normalQuantile`,
> `studentTCDF`/`studentTQuantile`, `chiSquaredCDF`/`chiSquaredQuantile`, `fCDF`/`fQuantile`,
> `gammaCDF`/`gammaQuantile`, `betaCDF`/`betaQuantile` (`distribution-functions.ts`, thin
> wrappers over the existing distribution objects) and `fTest`, `jarqueBera`
> (`hypothesis-extra.ts`, reusing `variance` + Wave A `skewness`/`kurtosis`).
>
> **Wave B (part 2) ✅ landed (2026-06-30).** Rank-based + exact tests, SciPy-verified
> (6/6): `rankdata` (the reusable ranking primitive — a P2 stats gap itself),
> `kruskalWallis` (tie-corrected, reuses `rankdata` + `chiSquaredCDF`), `wilcoxon`
> (signed-rank, normal approx + continuity correction, reuses `rankdata` + `normalCDF`),
> `fisherExact` (2×2, stable log-hypergeometric via `lgamma`). **`tukeyHSD` deferred** —
> it needs the studentized-range (q) distribution, which the library does not yet have;
> add that distribution first (Wave C/D).
>
> **Wave C (part 1) ✅ landed (2026-06-30).** Structured-matrix constructors + logdet
> (bridges C2/C5) and geodesy (C7), all NumPy/SciPy-verified (10/10): `tril`, `triu`,
> `vander`, `toeplitz`, `circulant`, `companion`, `logdet` (`linalg-extra.ts`, `logdet`
> reuses the matrix LU) and `haversine` (`geometry-extra.ts`, reuses `toRadians`).
> **Bug found + fixed along the way:** the `companion` cross-check (eigenvalues should
> be the polynomial roots) revealed that the mathjs factory `eigs` returns **wrong
> eigenvalues for every non-symmetric matrix** — even upper-triangular ones (diagonal
> eigenvalues). Root-caused and fixed: the public `eigs` now routes numeric square
> matrices through the native orthes/hqr2 solver (correct for symmetric/non-symmetric/
> complex spectra; regression in `eigs-correctness.test.ts`). Remaining Wave C: `hessian`,
> graph `laplacianMatrix` + spectral, `slerp`/`quaternion`. Wave D remains.

## Executive Summary

MathTS already has unusually deep coverage for a TypeScript library — the special
functions, linear-algebra decompositions, signal processing, interpolation, and
symbolic CAS surfaces rival SciPy in breadth. The gaps cluster in three places:

1. **Descriptive & inferential statistics** — the thinnest major domain relative to
   its importance (no `skewness`/`kurtosis`/`cov`/`gmean`/rank-correlation, narrow
   hypothesis-test set). High leverage, low effort.
2. **Cross-domain connectors** — the special-function primitives exist (`betainc`,
   `gammainc`, `erfcScalar`) but aren't surfaced as the standalone distribution/test
   helpers that depend on them; `hessian` is missing between calculus↔optimization;
   graph↔linalg (spectral) and geometry↔trig (geodesy) bridges are absent.
3. **Distribution & test breadth** — ~12 distributions vs SciPy's ~100; the
   hypothesis-test set omits several first-tier tests (`fTest`, `kruskalWallis`,
   `wilcoxon`, `fisherExact`).

## Part 1 — Within-domain function gaps

Priority key: **P1** high-leverage / low-effort · **P2** valuable · **P3** niche.

### 1.1 Arithmetic & elementary
- **P1 `logsumexp`** — numerically stable `log(Σ exp(xᵢ))`. Backbone of log-probability
  math; bridges arithmetic↔statistics↔ML. (`exp`/`log`/`max`/`sum` all exist — this is
  a thin, high-value composition that must be done *stably*.)
- **P1 `sigmoid`/`logistic`, `softmax`, `logSigmoid`** — ML/stats staples.
- **P1 `cumprod`, `cummax`, `cummin`** — `cumsum` exists; the family is incomplete.
- **P2 `clamp`/`clip`** — clamp to `[lo, hi]` (`isBounded` tests, doesn't clamp).
- **P3 IEEE/float ops** — `copysign`, `nextafter`, `frexp`, `ldexp`, `fma`.

### 1.2 Trigonometry
- **P1 `sinc`** — `sin(πx)/(πx)`; ubiquitous in signal processing (bridge trig↔signal).
- **P2 `haversine`** — see Geometry/geodesy (bridge trig↔geometry).

### 1.3 Special functions
- **P1 `polygamma(n, x)`** — `digamma` is only order 0; `trigamma` etc. needed for
  variance of log-gamma, distribution fitting.
- **P2 hypergeometric `₂F₁` (Gauss), `₁F₁` (Kummer), `pFq`** — many special functions
  and distribution CDFs are special cases; a foundational gap.
- **P2 spherical Bessel `jₙ`/`yₙ`** — physics (scattering, multipole expansions).
- **P2 `betaln`** — log-Beta for numerically stable PMFs (binomial, beta-binomial).
- **P2 `dawson`, Faddeeva `w(z)`** — Voigt profiles (spectroscopy), plasma dispersion.
- **P3 `hurwitzZeta`, `polylog`/`dilog` (Spence)**, Jacobi theta, `struveH`/`struveL`.

### 1.4 Combinatorics & number theory
- **P2 `stirlingS1`** — first-kind Stirling numbers (`stirlingS2` exists; asymmetric).
- **P2 `prevPrime`** — `nextPrime` exists; the pair is incomplete.
- **P2 `continuedFraction`/`convergents`** — bridge number-theory↔rational approximation
  (`rationalize` exists as the consumer).
- **P3 modular `sqrt` (Tonelli–Shanks), `primitiveRoot`, `multiplicativeOrder`,
  `discreteLog`, `kroneckerSymbol`** — computational number theory / crypto.

### 1.5 Statistics — *the highest-leverage domain*
- **P1 `skewness`, `kurtosis`** — third/fourth standardized moments; table-stakes
  descriptive stats, currently absent.
- **P1 `moment(x, k, {central})`** — general raw/central moments (generalizes the above).
- **P1 `cov`/`covariance` (pairwise + matrix), `corrcoef` (correlation matrix)** —
  `corr` is scalar Pearson only; the matrix forms bridge stats↔linalg (and underpin PCA,
  which exists but rebuilds them privately).
- **P1 `gmean`, `hmean`** — geometric/harmonic means.
- **P1 `iqr`, `zscore`/`standardize`, `sem`** — spread, standardization, standard error.
- **P2 `spearman`, `kendall`** — rank correlations (bridge stats↔nonparametric; the
  ranking machinery already exists inside `mannWhitneyTest`).
- **P2 `ecdf`** — empirical CDF (bridge stats↔distributions, feeds KS test).
- **P2 `weightedMean`, `trimMean`/`winsorize`, `rankdata`, `bincount`/`histogram`
  (non-parallel)**.

### 1.6 Probability distributions
- **P1 Distribution breadth** — add `cauchy`, `laplace`, `logistic`, `pareto`,
  `rayleigh`, `gumbel`/GEV, `negativeBinomial`, `hypergeometric`, `triangular`,
  `vonMises`. (Today ~12; the object API `dist(...).{pdf,cdf,quantile,sample}` is good —
  extend the *catalog*, not the shape.)
- **P1 `multivariateNormal`** — PDF + sampling; bridge distributions↔linalg (needs
  `logdet`/Cholesky, both adjacent).
- **P2 Standalone-function symmetry** — `normalCDF` exists as a free function but
  `normalQuantile` does not (only `normalDist(...).quantile`). Either expose the free
  `*Quantile`/`*CDF` set consistently, or document the object API as canonical.
- **P2 Distribution fitting (`fit`, MLE)** — bridge distributions↔optimization.

### 1.7 Linear algebra
- **P1 Structured-matrix constructors — `vander`, `toeplitz`, `hankel`, `circulant`,
  `companion`, `tril`/`triu`, `blockDiag`** — small, high-utility, and they are bridges
  (Vandermonde↔interpolation, circulant↔FFT, companion↔polynomial roots).
- **P1 `logdet`** — stable log-determinant; bridge linalg↔stats (Gaussian likelihoods).
- **P2 Generalized eigenproblem `A x = λ B x` / `qz`** — needed for vibration modes,
  GEV, LDA.
- **P2 `orth`/column space** (`nullspace` exists; the complement doesn't).
- **P3 Matrix trig `cosm`/`sinm`**, `khatriRao`, banded/Toeplitz fast solvers.

### 1.8 Vector calculus
- **P1 `hessian`** (symbolic + numeric) — *the* missing calculus↔optimization↔linalg
  bridge; second-order optimizers and Laplace approximations need it. `jacobian` and
  `gradientSymbolic` exist; the second-order object does not.
- **P2 numeric `gradient`** on sampled arrays (à la `numpy.gradient`) — distinct from
  the AD `gradientAt`; bridge calculus↔data/signal.
- **P3 line/surface/flux integrals.**

### 1.9 CAS
- **P2 Symbolic integration** — `integrate` is numerical; a symbolic antiderivative
  (even Risch-lite / table + parts + substitution) is a major SymPy capability gap.
- **P2 Symbolic ODE solve (`dsolve`)** — `solveODE` is numerical only.
- **P3 Symbolic linear solve / `linsolve` over symbols.**

### 1.10 Numerical integration
- **P1 `cumtrapz`** — cumulative integral (bridge integration↔signal; pairs with the
  missing `cumprod` family).
- **P2 Multi-dimensional `dblquad`/`tplquad`/`nquad`**, **Monte-Carlo `mcIntegrate`**
  (bridge integration↔probability).

### 1.11 Interpolation
- **P2 Regular-grid `interpn`/`interp2d`** — `griddata` handles scattered data; the
  fast regular-grid path is missing.
- **P3 `akima` spline, barycentric interpolation.**

### 1.12 Optimization & ODE
- **P1 Nonlinear least squares — `levenbergMarquardt`/`gaussNewton`** — `curvefit`
  exists but the canonical LM solver isn't exposed; bridge optimization↔curve-fitting.
- **P2 Named optimizers** — `nelderMead`, `bfgs`/`lbfgs`, `conjugateGradient`,
  `gradientDescent` (today only generic `minimize`/`globalMinimize`).
- **P2 Nonlinear system `fsolve`** — multivariate root finding (single-var `findRoot`
  exists).

### 1.13 Signal processing
- **P1 Filter *design*** — `butter`, `cheby1/2`, `ellip`, `bessel` (IIR) and `firwin`,
  `firls`, `remez`/Parks–McClellan (FIR). Today filters *apply* (`lowpassFilter` etc.)
  and `zpk2tf` exists, but coefficient design is missing.
- **P1 `filtfilt`** (zero-phase), `sosfilt`.
- **P2 `savgol`** (Savitzky–Golay — bridge signal↔polynomial fitting), `findPeaks`,
  `cepstrum`, `coherence`/`csd` (bridge signal↔stats), `istft`, `cwt`.

### 1.14 Geometry
- **P1 Geodesy — `haversine`/great-circle, `slerp`** (bridge geometry↔trig).
- **P2 `quaternion` algebra** (compose/normalize/toMatrix; bridge geometry↔algebra),
  `circumcircle`/`incircle`, `barycentricCoordinates` (bridge geometry↔interpolation),
  `boundingBox`/`boundingSphere`.
- **P3 polygon boolean ops (clip/union/intersection), Minkowski sum.**

### 1.15 Graph theory
- **P1 `maxFlow`/`minCut`** (Dinic/Edmonds–Karp), **bipartite matching** (Hungarian /
  Hopcroft–Karp; bridge graph↔optimization).
- **P1 Graph Laplacian + spectral methods** — `laplacianMatrix`, Fiedler vector,
  `spectralClustering` (bridge graph↔linalg↔stats).
- **P2 `degreeCentrality`, `closenessCentrality`, `clusteringCoefficient`/transitivity**
  (betweenness/eigenvector/pagerank exist; the set is partial).
- **P3 graph coloring, maximal cliques, community detection.**

### 1.16 Hypothesis tests
- **P1 `fTest` (variance ratio), `kruskalWallis`, `wilcoxonSignedRank`, `fisherExact`** —
  first-tier tests absent.
- **P2 `levene`/`bartlett` (variance homogeneity), `jarqueBera` (bridge tests↔skew/
  kurtosis), `andersonDarling`, p-value-bearing `pearsonr`/`spearmanr`,
  multiple-comparison correction (`bonferroni`/`fdr`), `tukeyHSD` (post-hoc for the
  existing `anova`).**

### 1.17 Time series (near-absent domain)
- **P2 `movingAverage`/`rollingMean`/`rollingStd`, `ewma`, `detrend`, statistical
  `acf`/`pacf`** — distinct from the signal-domain `autoCorrelation`; bridge signal↔stats.
- **P3 decomposition (`seasonalDecompose`), Holt–Winters, ARIMA.**

### 1.18 Regression / ML primitives
- **P1 `linearRegression`/`ols`** (coefficients + R²/residuals/std-errors) — a thin,
  high-value wrapper over `leastSquares` that bridges stats↔linalg.
- **P2 Regularized regression (ridge/Tikhonov, lasso), `logisticRegression`.**

## Part 2 — Cross-domain bridge gaps (the connector functions)

These are functions whose *value is precisely that they join two domains*. Several
have both endpoints already present — only the connector is missing.

| # | Bridge | Missing connector | Both endpoints exist? | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Arithmetic ↔ Stats/ML | `logsumexp`, `softmax`, `sigmoid` | yes (exp/log/sum/max) | **P1** |
| C2 | Stats ↔ Linear algebra | `cov`/`corrcoef` matrix, `logdet` | yes (PCA rebuilds them privately) | **P1** |
| C3 | Calculus ↔ Optimization | `hessian` | yes (`jacobian`, `minimize`) | **P1** |
| C4 | Special fns ↔ Distributions | standalone `*CDF`/`*Quantile` over `betainc`/`gammainc` | **yes — primitives present, not surfaced** | **P1** |
| C5 | Linear algebra ↔ Interp/Signal | `vander`, `toeplitz`, `circulant`, `companion` | partial (consumers exist) | P2 |
| C6 | Graph ↔ Linear algebra | graph `laplacianMatrix` + spectral clustering | yes (`eigs`, adjacency) | P2 |
| C7 | Geometry ↔ Trig (geodesy) | `haversine`, `slerp`, `quaternion` | yes (distance, rotation) | P2 |
| C8 | Distributions ↔ Optimization | distribution `fit` (MLE) | yes (`minimize`, dist pdfs) | P2 |
| C9 | Tests ↔ Descriptive stats | `jarqueBera` (needs skew/kurtosis), `tukeyHSD` (needs anova) | partial | P2 |
| C10 | Integration ↔ Probability | `mcIntegrate` (Monte-Carlo) | yes (`random`, integrators) | P2 |
| C11 | Signal ↔ Polynomial fit | `savgol` (Savitzky–Golay) | yes (`polyFit`, convolve) | P2 |
| C12 | Number theory ↔ Approximation | `continuedFraction`/`convergents` | yes (`rationalize` consumes) | P3 |

**The standout is C4.** The hard parts — regularized incomplete beta (`betainc`),
incomplete gamma (`gammainc`), and the accurate `erfcScalar`/Acklam quantile machinery
(added in the GC3 correctness work) — already exist and already back the distribution
*objects*. Exposing the thin standalone `chi2CDF`/`studentTCDF`/`fCDF`/`gammaCDF`/
`betaCDF` + their quantiles would close most of the distribution-function surface and
directly unblock the missing hypothesis tests (C9), since `fTest`/`kruskalWallis`/etc.
need exactly those tail probabilities.

## Part 3 — Prioritized roadmap (by leverage ÷ effort)

**Wave A — descriptive-stats + arithmetic primitives (small, pure, high-traffic):**
`skewness`, `kurtosis`, `moment`, `cov`/`corrcoef`, `gmean`/`hmean`, `iqr`, `zscore`,
`logsumexp`, `softmax`/`sigmoid`, `cumprod`/`cummax`/`cummin`, `cumtrapz`. Each is a
few lines, pure-numeric, and testable against a NumPy/SciPy oracle (the audit harness
in `tools/math-correctness-audit/` already exists).

**Wave B — distribution/test surfacing (reuses existing special-fn primitives):**
standalone `*CDF`/`*Quantile` (C4) → then `fTest`, `kruskalWallis`, `wilcoxon`,
`fisherExact`, `jarqueBera`, `tukeyHSD` (C9). Highest *capability unlocked per line*.

**Wave C — structural bridges:** `hessian` (C3), `vander`/`toeplitz`/`circulant`/
`companion` + `tril`/`triu`/`logdet` (C2/C5), graph `laplacianMatrix` + spectral (C6),
geodesy `haversine`/`slerp`/`quaternion` (C7).

**Wave D — heavier domains:** filter design (`butter`/`firwin`/`filtfilt`), named
optimizers + `levenbergMarquardt`, generalized eig/`qz`, distribution breadth, symbolic
integration, time-series basics, `linearRegression`/regularized regression.

**Wave A is the recommended start:** lowest risk, highest daily-use frequency, and it
closes the single most under-developed domain (statistics) relative to its importance.

## Methodology & honesty notes

- **Grounding (Rule 4):** the inventory is the generated `functions.md` export index
  (744 exports). A scripted probe of 70+ candidate names against the live
  `functions/dist` confirmed each listed gap is genuinely absent (not exported under an
  alternate name). Borderline cases were checked directly.
- **Correction made during analysis:** the distribution *objects*
  (`normalDist(0,1)` etc.) **do** expose `.pdf`/`.cdf`/`.quantile`/`.sample`/`.sampleN`
  — so quantile/sampling capability is *not* missing; the distributions gap is catalog
  *breadth* and free-function *symmetry*, not capability. Stated accordingly above.
- **Scope:** coverage percentages are judgment calls against the SciPy/SymPy bar, meant
  for prioritization, not precision. "Missing" means "not a public export of
  `@danielsimonjr/mathts-functions`"; a few items may exist as private helpers inside
  another function (noted where known, e.g. ranking inside `mannWhitneyTest`).
- **Relation to prior reports:** package-level bridges (compat, workbook, tensor↔autograd)
  and mathjs-name parity are tracked in the other two roadmap docs and are deliberately
  out of scope here.
