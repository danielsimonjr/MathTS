`eig` and `eigvals` of a zero matrix (3x3 and larger) no longer throw `RangeError: eig: QR iteration did not converge`, and the eigenvectors of the 2x2 zero matrix are no longer NaN. The Hessenberg deflation test now accepts an exactly zero sub-diagonal when the norm is zero. A sub-diagonal entry exactly equal to `eps * s` now also deflates. For such an input the eigenvalues can differ in the last bits and in order, and the eigenvectors of a repeated eigenvalue can be a different valid set. For `[[1,0,0],[2^-51,1,0],[0,3,2]]` the eigenvalues change from `[0.9999999999999998, 2, 1]` to `[1, 1, 2]`; the residual stays at rounding level. Fixes #1192.
