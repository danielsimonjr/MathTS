`eig` and `eigvals` of a zero matrix (3x3 and larger) no longer throw `RangeError: eig: QR iteration did not converge`, and the eigenvectors of the 2x2 zero matrix are no longer NaN. The Hessenberg deflation test now accepts an exactly zero sub-diagonal when the norm is zero. Fixes #1192.
