`eig` and `eigvals` of a zero matrix (3x3 and larger) no longer throw `RangeError: eig: QR iteration did not converge`, and the eigenvectors of the 2x2 zero matrix are no longer NaN. The Hessenberg deflation test now accepts an exactly zero sub-diagonal when the norm is zero. A sub-diagonal entry exactly equal to `eps * s` now also deflates, so such inputs can return eigenvalues and eigenvectors that differ in the last bits or in order. Fixes #1192.
