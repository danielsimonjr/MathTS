BigNumber handling fixes (Fixes #1117, #1119, #1120).

- #1117: a Fraction converts to a BigNumber as numerator / denominator at the configured precision, not through a 16-digit number. `add(fraction(1, 3), bignumber(1))` gave 1.3333333333333333 and now gives 1.333... to 64 digits. `quantileSeq([fraction(1, 3), fraction(2, 3), fraction(1)], bignumber(3))` gave 0.49999999999999995 and 0.8333333333333333 for the outer quantiles and now gives 0.5 and 0.8333... to 64 digits. A BigNumber method that takes a Fraction (`BigNumber.add(fraction)`) converts the same way. This departs from mathjs 15.2.0 on purpose: mathjs throws a TypeError for `add(fraction, bignumber)`; MathTS keeps its implicit conversion and now keeps the digits. `bignumber(fraction)` already matched mathjs.
- #1119: `bignumber()` returns 0, as mathjs. `bignumber(undefined)` throws a TypeError that names `bignumber` (mathjs also throws; the old error was a raw "reading 'isMatrix'"). `bignumber(complex(1, 2))` and `bignumber({})` throw a TypeError, as mathjs (they gave 2.236... and NaN). `bignumber('0x1F')`, `'0b101'`, `'0o17'`, a hex fraction and the word-size form `'0xFFi8'` parse as in mathjs (they threw a SyntaxError).
- #1120: `solveODE` with `RK23` or `RK45` and a BigNumber `tspan` or `y0` uses the same first-step heuristic as the number path (one shared function). For `y' = -y` on [0, 1], RK23 gave y(1) = 1/3 in 2 steps and now gives 0.3679 (e^-1) in many steps. This departs from mathjs 15.2.0 on purpose: mathjs returns 0.3333333333333334 in 2 steps for the same call.
