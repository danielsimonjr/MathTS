`solve`, `solveExact`, `integrate`, `expand` and `coefficientTexts` close ten follow-up items of #965. A power of a base that cancels to a constant (`(x-x)^1000000000`) is an identity, and `solve('sin(x)-sin(x)')` reports an identity. The numeric scan finds the root of `(x-1)^2` between two grid points. The radicals of a quadratic have one text and one order (`x^2-0.5` gives `-sqrt(2)/2` and `sqrt(2)/2`). The root of `1e-20*x-1e-30` is `1e-10`. `solveExact('sin(x)=sqrt(2)/3')` gives `asin(sqrt(2)/3)`, and `solveExact` solves a polynomial of degree 1 or 2. `integrate('5*x^3')` is `5*x^4/4`. `expand('(x+y+z+w)^900')` is refused by its term count. `coefficientTexts` writes each fraction on its own, so `[0.5, 0.25, 2]` gives `['1/2', '1/4', '2']`. This changes output text: `expand('0.5*x*(x+1)')` is now `1/2*x^2 + 1/2*x` (it was `0.5*x^2 + 0.5*x`), which reverses the decimal rule of #887. A coefficient below `1e-14` stays a decimal and is no longer written as `0/1`. (Refs #966)
