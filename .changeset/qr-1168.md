Fixes #1168. `qr` of a small matrix no longer loses a column. The Householder step reads a residual column norm of 1e-15 or less as zero, an absolute test. A matrix with a largest entry between 2^-20 and 1 was not scaled, so the residual of a nearly dependent column fell below the test and the reflection was skipped. `qr` now scales every matrix with a largest entry below 1 up by an exact power of 2 and scales R back. Core exports the window start `UNIT_FLOOR_LO` (2^0) from `/internal`. This is a new internal export, so the export lock is refreshed on purpose with `node tools/test/check-export-lock.mjs --write`.

Value changes: the factors of a matrix with a largest entry in [2^-20, 1) and a column that is within about 1e-15 (absolute) of the span of earlier columns change from a skipped step to the true reflection. `[[1,1,1],[1,1+3e-10,1],[1,1,1+3e-10]]·2^-19` had a reconstruction error of 5e-11 and a wrong sign of R22; the error is now about 1e-16. Every other input gives the same bits as before.
