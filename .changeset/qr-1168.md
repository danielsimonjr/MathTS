Fixes #1168. `qr` of a small matrix of plain numbers no longer loses a column. The Householder step reads a residual column norm of 1e-15 or less as zero, an absolute test. A matrix with a largest entry in [2^-20, 1) was not scaled, so the residual of a nearly dependent column fell below the test and the reflection was skipped. `qr` now scales every all-number matrix with a largest entry below 1 by an exact power of 2 into [1, 2), and scales R back. Core exports the new window exponent `UNIT_FLOOR_LO = 0` (window start 2^0) from `/internal`. Complex, BigNumber and Fraction matrices are not scaled and still have the defect (#1188).

Value changes: the factors of an all-number matrix with a largest entry in [2^-20, 1) and a column within about 1e-15 (absolute) of the span of earlier columns change from a skipped step to the true reflection. `[[1,1,1],[1,1+3e-10,1],[1,1,1+3e-10]]·2^-19` had a reconstruction error of 5e-11 and a wrong sign of R22; the error is now about 3e-16. Scaling by a power of 2 is exact, so other inputs give the same factors, except where an entry is subnormal before or after scaling.
