Opt-in physics mode: bare `e` is the elementary charge 1.602176634e-19 C when `evaluate`, `compileExpr`, or `parser` is called with `{ physics: true }`, or when the scope comes from `physicsScope()`. The default stays Euler's number. `E` stays unbound for energy, and Euler's number is `exp(1)`.
