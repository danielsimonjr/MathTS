Kernels of the matrix package no longer compare data to an absolute constant that depends on the unit of the matrix (Fixes #1123, #1125, #1127).

Root causes and results (SciPy and NumPy 2.3.5 are the oracle):

- `WASMBackend.qrDecomposition`, `luDecomposition` and `inverse` (JS and AssemblyScript paths) skipped a column or a pivot below an absolute 1e-14 and squared entries above 1e154. The three methods now run on a copy scaled by an exact power of 2 when the largest entry is outside [2^-3, 2^400]. `[[3e-100,1e-100],[4e-100,2e-100]]` gave `Q = I, R = A` and `singular: true`; it now gives `|R| = [[5, 2.2], [0, 0.4]]e-100`, a regular LU and `inverse = [[1e100, -5e99], [-2e100, 1.5e100]]`. `[[3e200,1e200],[4e200,2e200]]` gave `Q·R ≠ A`; it now gives `Qᵀ·R = A` to 1e-16. The window constant is `UNIT_LO` in `core/src/pow2-scale.ts`; the scaling uses the shared `withPow2Scale`.
- `matrixSqrtm` compared with an absolute 1e-8 (symmetry, negative eigenvalue), 1e-7 (complex eigenvalue), 1e-13 (Newton step) and 1e-14 (2×2 block). Below a largest entry of 1 each cutoff is now relative to it. At or above 1 the cutoff is unchanged, so an ordinary matrix keeps its result. `matrixSqrtm([[4,1],[1.02,3]]·2^-22)` gave a symmetric matrix with off-diagonal 1.3359e-4; it now gives 1.3227e-4 and 1.3492e-4 (SciPy). `matrixSqrtm(zeros(2))` gave `5.684e-14·I`; it now gives zeros (SciPy).
- `matrixSqrtm` of a non-symmetric matrix was wrong from about 2^40 (#1127). The Schur form keeps Hessenberg residue below its quasi-diagonal of up to 1e-12 of the neighbouring diagonal, and the absolute test `|T[i+1][i]| < 1e-12` read residue of a large matrix as a 2×2 block. The test is now relative to the neighbouring diagonal. For G·2^52 the error of the root, scaled back, was 0.0122; it is now 0 (equal to `sqrtm(G)`), and G·2^300 gave NaN and now gives the root.
- `matrixLogm` rejected an eigenvalue at or below an absolute 1e-14. The Schur path now accepts such an eigenvalue only when it is above 1e-14 of the smallest row scale (when that scale is below 1) and 1e8 times above the backward error `max|A - Q·T·Qᵀ|` of the Schur form, so its logarithm has a relative error of at most 1e-8. `matrixLogm(diag(1, 1e-20))` threw `non-positive eigenvalue`; it now gives `diag(0, -46.0517)` (SciPy). The Schur block test and the root denominator test are relative the same way.
- `qr` treated a column of residual norm below an absolute 1e-14 as dependent. The cutoff is now relative to the largest entry when that entry is below 1. A 3-column matrix of pivots 1.6e-15 and 1.3e-15 at 2^-19 kept its column in the NumPy values.

Value and throw changes (no other result changes for an ordinary matrix):

- `matrixSqrtm` of a matrix of large entries that was rejected as having complex eigenvalues (imaginary part above the absolute 1e-7) now returns the root (2 of 700 scaled cases; the error is 1e-14).
- `matrixLogm` of a matrix with a small eigenvalue that the Schur form resolves to 1e-8 now returns a value instead of throwing `non-positive eigenvalue` (`diag(1, 1e-20)` and 4 more `diag(1, tiny)`, 2 of 250 graded matrices; relative error 2e-13 to 1.4e-11 against an 80-digit reference). The 37 other graded matrices, which resolve a small eigenvalue worse than 1e-8 (relative error up to 0.2 if returned), and every rank-deficient dense matrix still throw.
- `WASMBackend.luDecomposition` reported `singular: true` for a regular matrix with a largest entry below 2^-3, or with a pivot between 1e-14 and its scale. It reports `false` now (50 of 700 scaled and 8 of 250 graded cases).

Differential against the previous release (1,600 ordinary matrices per function of size 1 to 6; 700 scaled by 2^-60 to 2^600; 250 graded `D·A·D`, 80-digit mpmath for the graded cases): bit-equal means the same doubles.

| function                                    | ordinary bit-equal | scaled                                                                                               | graded                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `matrixSqrtm`                               | 1600 of 1600       | 661 equal, 10 closer, 16 equal error, 11 further within 4x of an error under 1e-14, 2 throw to value | 246 equal, 2 closer               |
| `matrixLogm`                                | 1601 of 1601       | 684 equal, 8 closer, 1 equal error, 7 further within 4x of an error under 5e-12                      | 248 equal, 2 throw to value       |
| `qr`                                        | 1600 of 1600       | 700 of 700                                                                                           | 248 equal, 1 closer               |
| `WASMBackend` `qr` (AS and JS)              | 1600 of 1600 each  | 626 equal, 74 closer                                                                                 | 243 to 246 equal, the rest closer |
| `WASMBackend` `luDecomposition` (AS and JS) | 1600 of 1600 each  | 650 equal, 50 singular to regular                                                                    | 242 equal, 8 singular to regular  |
| `WASMBackend` `inverse` (AS and JS)         | 1600 of 1600 each  | 650 equal, 50 closer (was zeros)                                                                     | 238 to 249 equal, the rest closer |

No result is further from the oracle than the previous release by more than 4 times an error under 1e-14, except the throw to value cases above.
