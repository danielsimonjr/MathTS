`symbolicIntegral` now integrates a power of `sin(a*x+b)` or `cos(a*x+b)` (integer power 2 to 64) and a polynomial times `log(a*x+b)`. `laplace` now applies linearity, the shift theorem and `t^n*exp(a*t)`. `inverseLaplace` now reads a rational function of `s` by partial fractions (real poles with multiplicity, one complex pair). An input that none of these rules covers keeps the `integral(...)` marker in `symbolicIntegral` (the marker parses as a call). `laplace` and `inverseLaplace` now throw the new exported `NoClosedFormError` for such an input; they returned the text `L{...}(s)` or `L^-1{...}(t)`, which does not parse. A rational function with two real poles too close to separate (`1/(s^2-1e-14)`) throws `NoClosedFormError` in `inverseLaplace`, and `inverseLaplaceTransform` throws an `Error` for it; both gave `t`. The sampled table of `inverseLaplaceTransform` compares values with a relative tolerance, so `1/(s-1e-9)` is no longer read as `1/s`. A `log` with a base (`x*log(x, 2)`) keeps the marker; it gave the antiderivative of the natural logarithm. (Refs #1024)
