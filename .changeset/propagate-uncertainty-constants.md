`propagateUncertainty` takes CODATA constants in `values`: the expression reads the SI magnitude of each constant, and its `.uncertainty` in SI is the sigma of that input (#910). The central-difference step starts at `relativeStep * max(|x|, sigma)` and then shrinks (when `f` overflows) or grows (when the difference is within rounding of `f`), so `exp(1e10 * x)` at `x = 1e-20` and at `0` give the right partial; the old `1e-30` floor gave `1e72` for `planckTime` and 0 for `exp` at 0. The result lists an input whose partial stays within rounding of 0 in `flat`. A covariance that leaves a constant's own uncertainty out (zero diagonal) throws and names the input. A positional callback `(m, c) => ...` throws an error that names the record form `({m, c}) => ...`. A non-finite sigma throws a `RangeError`, and the negative-variance tolerance is relative to the scale of the quadratic form. A `Unit` passed as `values` throws a `TypeError` that names the expected shape. The JSDoc of `evaluate` states which symbols (`G`, `hbar`, `c`, `h`) the default scope reads as units and which `{ physics: true }` binds as constants (#908, #909).
