The polynomial reader of the CAS has a limit on the number of terms: 10000 (`MAX_POLYNOMIAL_TERMS`, next to the degree limit 1000). A product that holds more than 10000 distinct terms makes `expand` throw a `PolynomialSizeError` (a `RangeError`) with the message `expand: the polynomial has more than 10000 terms, the most that the CAS builds`. Before the change, `expand('(a+b+c+d+x)^21')` (12650 terms) took seconds, `(a+b+c+d+x)^40` took 28 seconds, and `(a+b+c+d+x)^60` did not end in 100 seconds. Now each of them ends at once with the error. The count is of distinct terms, not of products, so a dense univariate product still works. `expand('(a+b+c+d+x)^19')` (8855 terms) and `expand('(x+1)^1000')` (1001 terms) give the same result as before. An input above the degree limit still gives the degree error. `groebnerBasis` and the elimination of a variable refuse such an input in the same way. `factor`, `together`, `apart` and the integrators read a univariate polynomial of at most 1001 terms, or catch the error and use their other path. (Refs #1176)
