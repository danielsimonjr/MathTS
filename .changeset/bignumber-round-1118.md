`BigNumber` `add`, `subtract` and `multiply` round the result to `config.precision` with the configured rounding mode, as decimal.js and mathjs do. Before, the square of a 64-digit value had 130 digits, and `add(bignumber('1'), bignumber('1e-30'))` at precision 20 gave `1.000000000000000000000000000001` (now `1`). A loop such as `solveODE` no longer grows without bound (29,005 digits after 24 steps). An integer `pow` runs its products with guard digits and rounds once. Constructors, `negate` and `abs` keep every digit. Integer arithmetic beyond the precision loses digits, as in mathjs and decimal.js: `add(bignumber('1e30'), bignumber('1'))` at precision 20 gives 10^30 (the 1 is lost), and `factorial(bignumber(60))` at precision 64 has 64 significant digits. `mod` is exact and does not round, so `gcd` and the floored factory `mod` stay exact for integers longer than the precision. `isPrime` raises the precision for its Miller-Rabin products. The release is a patch because the documented contract is `config.precision`. Results of more than `precision` digits were a defect, not a feature (Fixes #1118).
