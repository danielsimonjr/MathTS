`eigs` decides that a computed eigenvalue is real with a tolerance relative to the matrix: `|im| <= 1e-10 * max|A_ij|`. The old tolerance was the absolute number `1e-10`. Fixes #1186.

- A matrix of small scale keeps its complex pair. `eigs([[0, -1e-150], [1e-150, 0]])` returns `-1e-150i` and `1e-150i`, with eigenvectors that fit. It returned the real `0` twice, with a residual of about 1.
- A matrix of large scale (`max|A_ij|` above 1) now treats a relative imaginary part up to `1e-10` as real. A pair with a relative imaginary part of `1e-12` at scale `1e6` returns two real values. It returned a complex pair. The behaviour at scale 1 does not change.
