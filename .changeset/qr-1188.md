`qr` of a Complex, BigNumber, Fraction or mixed number/Complex matrix keeps a nearly dependent column. The Householder step skips a column only when its norm is exactly zero. The tolerance of `isZero` no longer applies, so a small matrix such as `[[1,1,1],[1,1+3e-10,1],[1,1,1+3e-10]] * 2^-19` reconstructs to rounding error instead of 5e-11. Fixes #1188.
