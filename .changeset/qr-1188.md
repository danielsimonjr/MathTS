`qr` of a Complex, BigNumber, Fraction or mixed number/Complex matrix keeps a nearly dependent column. For a Complex, BigNumber or mixed matrix, the Householder step skips a column only when its norm is exactly zero, so the tolerance of `isZero` no longer drops it. A small matrix such as `[[1,1,1],[1,1+3e-10,1],[1,1,1+3e-10]] * 2^-19` now reconstructs to rounding error instead of 5e-11. A Fraction matrix is converted to numbers and is scaled like a number matrix. A number or Complex matrix is scaled by an exact power of 2 into [2^0, 2^400] (the largest real or imaginary part), so a Complex matrix at 1e-300 or 1e300 no longer gives a NaN Q or a wrong R. A BigNumber matrix is not scaled. Fixes #1188.
