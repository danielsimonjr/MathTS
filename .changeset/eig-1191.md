`eig(A).isSymmetric` is true only when every `|a_ij - a_ji|` is at most `8 * n * eps * ||A||_F`, so it is exact up to the rounding of the input and does not depend on the scale of `A`. The test was `|a_ij - a_ji| / max|a| <= 1e-10`, so `[[1, 1e-12], [0, 1]]` and any other matrix whose asymmetry was above `8 * n * eps * ||A||_F` but at most 1e-10 relative to `max|a|` reported `true`; they report `false` now. The flag does not choose a solver or change the eigenvalues, vectors or `repeatedEigenvalues`: the result is bit-equal to the former one apart from the flag (Fixes #1191).
