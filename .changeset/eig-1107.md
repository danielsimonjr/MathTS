Fixes #1107: `eig` and `eigvals` at subnormal scale, and the `maxIterations` option.

- `eig([[1e-200,1e-200],[0,1e-200]])` reported `isSymmetric: true`, two independent eigenvectors in `repeatedEigenvalues` and `defective: false`. It now reports `isSymmetric: false`, one independent eigenvector and `defective: true`, as at scale 1 and scale 1e153. The symmetry test has an absolute tolerance, so it now reads the matrix at a fixed power-of-2 scale. `repeatedEigenvalues` now factors the matrix at the scale of the solver, and its `value` is scaled back to the scale of the input.
- `eig([[1,4e-323,0],[4e-323,4,4e-323],[0,4e-323,9]])` gave `Infinity` and `NaN` in `vectors`. They are now finite unit vectors. The cause was an overflow of `1 / scale` in the Hessenberg accumulation for a reflector with a subnormal scale.
- `eig` and `eigvals` throw a `RangeError` when `maxIterations` is not a positive integer (`NaN`, `null`, `0`, a negative number, a fraction, `Infinity`, a string). `NaN` switched the iteration limit off and `null` threw an unrelated error. An omitted `maxIterations` still means 1000.
