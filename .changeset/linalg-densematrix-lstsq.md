`@danielsimonjr/mathts-linalg` exports `DenseMatrix`. `lstsq(A, b)` is a new export of matrix, linalg and functions: the Householder QR least-squares solution of `A x ≈ b` for rows ≥ columns. The rectangular `lsolve` error names `lstsq`.
