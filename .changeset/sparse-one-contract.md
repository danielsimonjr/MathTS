The factory functions accept the public `SparseMatrix` of `@danielsimonjr/mathts-matrix` (#949). A function that declares a matrix type converts a public `SparseMatrix` argument on entry. About 100 signatures of `dotDivide`, `dotMultiply`, `compare`, `equal`, `gcd`, `atan2`, `ceil`, `floor`, `dotPow`, `nthRoot` and others no longer fail with `size is not a function`, `density is not a function` or `Cannot perform operation on Pattern Sparse Matrix and Scalar value`. The return class follows mathjs. Two sparse operands give a public `SparseMatrix`; this changes `add` (was dense) and `multiply` (was an Array) of two sparse operands. A number added to a sparse operand, and a comparison with a number, give a dense matrix. A boolean result of two sparse operands is a `SparseMatrix` of 1 and 0. A result with a Complex, BigNumber or Fraction entry is a nested Array. A signature that refuses a `SparseMatrix` throws an error that names `toDense()`. The public `equal`, `compare`, `larger`, `smaller`, `largerEq` and `smallerEq` stay scalar-only for every collection. `divide(number, matrix)` in the factory scope no longer throws `Class constructor Complex cannot be invoked without 'new'`.
