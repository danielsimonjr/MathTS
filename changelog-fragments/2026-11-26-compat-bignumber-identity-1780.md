### Fixed

- **Unified compat BigNumber class (#1780):** `math.bignumber()`, `new math.BigNumber()`, and compat function results normalize to the same `CompatBigNumber` class at the instance boundary (`compatDecimalResult` on lazy wrappers and `bindShim`). `valueOf()` returns the decimal string (mathjs 15.3.0 / decimal.js semantics), internal `_exponent` never stays `-0`, matrix/array leaves and expression `evaluate()` are wrapped, booleans promote when paired with a BigNumber, and instance `typed` converts `bigint` to `CompatBigNumber`. Bitwise BigNumber shifts follow mathjs infinite/negative-shift rules; `log10`/`log2` on `bigint` stay numbers when `number` is not `BigNumber`.
